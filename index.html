HELP & INFORMATION

Frequently asked
questions.

Can ceiling cloth drying systems be installed in POP or false ceilings?

Yes. Our technicians use extended anchor fasteners that securely hold into the main concrete slab above the POP/false ceiling without damaging your ceiling design.

How much weight can each drying rod hold?

Each rod is built to hold daily wet laundry including jeans, towels, and bedsheets comfortably. Because each rod operates on its own pulley rope, weight stays evenly balanced.

How do I get exact size options and a quote?

Simply tap any WhatsApp button on this page or call us at 9179622121. You can also send a quick photo of your balcony for an instant recommendation.

Do you also repair old cloth drying stands?

Yes, we provide rope replacement, pulley wheel replacement, and re-installation services for existing ceiling and wall-mounted cloth drying stands.
